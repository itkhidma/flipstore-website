Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

This Cancellation Policy explains when and how an order placed on FlipStore can be cancelled, by you or by us, and what happens to your payment afterwards. It forms part of our Terms of Service and should be read together with our Refund & Returns Policy.

1. Cancellation Window

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time until it is marked "Out for Delivery." Once an order is out for delivery, the cancellation option is no longer available in the app.

This means cancellation is available while an order is being confirmed, packed, or in transit, right up to the point it is handed to the delivery agent for the final leg. No cancellation fee applies within this window.

2. How to Cancel an Order

  • Open the FlipStore app and go to My Orders
  • Select the order you wish to cancel
  • Tap Cancel Order and choose a reason
  • You will receive an on-screen and email confirmation once the cancellation is registered

You may cancel an entire order or, where the order contains multiple items, individual items within it. Items that form part of a combo, bundle, or "buy one get one" offer must be cancelled together.

3. After the Cancellation Window

Once an order is out for delivery, it can no longer be cancelled in the app. You may instead:

  • Refuse delivery: Decline the package when the delivery agent arrives. The item will be returned to us and, for prepaid orders, treated as a return under our Refund & Returns Policy.
  • Accept and return: Accept the delivery and raise a return request, provided the item is eligible for return under its category window.

Mobile phones are non-refundable under the Refund & Returns Policy, except for verified damage, defects, or incorrect delivery. This restriction applies after delivery; a prepaid mobile-phone order cancelled before it is marked "Out for Delivery" is refunded under Section 5.1.

Repeatedly refusing delivery of Cash on Delivery orders may result in restrictions on that payment method for your account.

4. Non-Cancellable Orders

The following cannot be cancelled once the order is confirmed, because production or fulfilment begins immediately:

  • Personalised, customised, or made-to-order products
  • Digital products, e-vouchers, gift cards, and downloadable content, once delivered or redeemed
  • Fresh, frozen, and other perishable goods once picked and packed
  • Items clearly marked "Non-Cancellable" on the product page at the time of purchase

5. Refunds for Cancelled Orders

5.1 Prepaid Orders

Where payment has already been made, the full amount paid, including any shipping charges, is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds are initiated within 24 to 48 hours of the cancellation being confirmed.

Payment Method Time to Credit (after initiation)
UPI 1–3 business days
Credit or debit card 5–7 business days
Net banking 5–7 business days
Third-party wallets 3–5 business days

The time taken to credit your account after initiation is determined by your bank or payment provider. We will share a refund reference number you can use with them.

5.2 Cash on Delivery Orders

No payment is collected for cancelled Cash on Delivery orders, so no refund is due.

5.3 Failed and Pending Payments

If your payment was debited but the order was not created, the amount is usually auto-reversed by your bank within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not, contact us with your transaction reference and we will trace it with the payment gateway.

5.4 Coupons and Promotional Benefits

Single-use coupons and promotional discounts applied to a cancelled order will be reinstated to your account where the promotion is still valid. Expired promotions cannot be reinstated or exchanged for cash.

6. Cancellation by FlipStore or a Vendor

We or a vendor may cancel an order, in whole or in part, where:

  • The product is out of stock or has been discontinued
  • The listing contained a pricing, description, or specification error
  • The delivery address falls outside a serviceable area
  • The address or contact details provided are incomplete, incorrect, or unreachable
  • The order is flagged by our fraud, abuse, or bulk-purchase checks
  • Delivery is prevented by circumstances beyond our reasonable control

You will be notified by email or in-app notification, and any amount paid will be refunded in full to the original payment method under Section 5.1. We are not liable for any further loss arising from such a cancellation.

7. Delivery Attempts and Automatic Cancellation

We make up to three delivery attempts. If delivery cannot be completed after the third attempt, or if the package remains unclaimed, the order will be cancelled automatically and returned to origin. For prepaid orders, the amount paid will be refunded to the original payment method, less any shipping charges incurred where the failure resulted from incorrect information or unavailability at the delivery address.

8. Rescheduling Instead of Cancelling

If the timing rather than the order itself is the problem, you may be able to reschedule delivery from the order details screen. Rescheduling keeps the order active and avoids the need to place it again at a later price.

9. Subscription and Recurring Orders

Where FlipStore offers scheduled or recurring deliveries, you may cancel future instalments at any time from the subscription settings in the app. Cancellation takes effect from the next undelivered instalment; an instalment already picked and packed will be delivered as scheduled.

10. Misuse of Cancellation

We monitor cancellation patterns for misuse, including repeated cancellations after dispatch, cancellations intended to exploit pricing or promotional errors, and bulk ordering followed by cancellation. Where misuse is identified, we may decline future orders, restrict payment methods, or suspend the account in accordance with our Terms of Service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The policy in effect on the date your order was placed governs that order. Material changes will be notified in the app and reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
