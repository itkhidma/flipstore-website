Refund & Returns Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

This Refund & Returns Policy explains when you can return a product purchased through FlipStore, how refunds are calculated, and how long they take. It forms part of our Terms of Service and should be read together with our Cancellation Policy.

1. Return Eligibility

Important: Mobile phones are non-refundable and cannot be returned under this policy, except where they arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. Every other item is eligible for return within 10 days of delivery, subject to the conditions below.

To be eligible for a return, an item must be:

  • Unused, unworn, unwashed, and free from damage caused after delivery
  • In its original packaging with all tags, labels, manuals, accessories, and free gifts intact
  • Accompanied by the original invoice or order ID
  • Requested within 10 days of delivery, unless the item is a mobile phone

Returns are subject to a quality check on receipt. If an item fails the quality check, it will be sent back to you and no refund will be issued.

2. Return Window

Return windows are counted from the date of delivery:

Category Return Window Notes
Mobile phones Non-refundable Returns are accepted only for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect
All other items 10 days Subject to the eligibility requirements and exclusions in this policy

3. Non-Returnable Items

Mobile phones cannot be returned or refunded regardless of the reason for return, except for verified damage, defect, or incorrect delivery.

  • Mobile phones

4. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

IMPORTANT: Report any damaged, defective, missing, or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery (within 24 hours for groceries and perishables). Claims raised after this period may not be accepted.

When raising such a claim, please provide:

  • Your order ID and the affected item
  • Clear photographs of the product, its defects, and the outer packaging
  • An unboxing video, where available, for high-value and fragile items

Once verified, we will offer a replacement, a repair, or a full refund including any shipping charges paid. In these cases, return pickup is free of charge.

5. How to Request a Return

  • Open the FlipStore app and go to My Orders
  • Select the order and tap Return
  • Choose a reason, upload photographs if prompted, and submit the request
  • You will receive confirmation and pickup details once the request is approved

Where reverse pickup is unavailable at your PIN code, you may be asked to self-ship the item to the address we provide. Reasonable courier charges will be reimbursed on submission of the receipt.

6. Return Shipping Charges

  • Free pickup: Damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing items
  • Charged to you: Change of mind, size or colour preference, or ordering by mistake — the applicable return shipping fee will be deducted from your refund and shown before you confirm the request
  • Original outbound shipping charges are non-refundable unless the error was ours

7. Refunds

7.1 Refund Method

All approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the order. We do not issue refunds to alternative accounts, cards, or third-party payment instruments.

For Cash on Delivery orders, where a refund to the original payment method is not possible, the refund will be credited to a bank account in your name. You will be asked to provide the account holder name, account number, and IFSC code.

7.2 Refund Timelines

Refunds are approved within 2 business days of the returned item passing our quality check, and then initiated to your payment provider. Credit to your account typically takes:

Payment Method Time to Credit (after initiation)
UPI 1–3 business days
Credit or debit card 5–7 business days
Net banking 5–7 business days
Third-party wallets 3–5 business days
Cash on Delivery (bank transfer) 7–10 business days from receipt of bank details

Once a refund is initiated, the time taken to credit your account is determined by your bank or payment provider and is outside our control. We will share a refund reference number that you can use with them.

7.3 Refund Amount

Your refund will reflect the amount paid for the item, less:

  • Return shipping charges, where applicable under Section 6
  • Non-refundable outbound shipping and handling charges
  • The value of any discount, coupon, or promotional benefit that becomes invalid once the item is returned

Where a promotion required a minimum order value and a return brings the order below that value, the discount may be recovered from the refund.

8. Partial Returns and Combo Offers

If you return part of a multi-item order, only the value of the returned items is refunded. Items purchased as part of a combo, bundle, or "buy one get one" offer must be returned together; individual components of such offers cannot be returned separately.

9. Vendor Products

FlipStore operates as a multi-vendor marketplace. Returns and refunds for vendor-listed products are processed in line with this policy, and FlipStore coordinates the process on your behalf. Where a vendor disputes a return, FlipStore may intervene to resolve the matter fairly and reserves the right to make a final determination.

10. Failed or Rejected Refunds

If a refund fails because of incorrect bank details, a closed account, or an expired card, we will notify you and request updated details. Refunds that remain unclaimed for 180 days after repeated notification may be forfeited to the extent permitted by law.

11. Fraud and Abuse

We monitor return activity for misuse, including returning items other than those delivered, excessive or serial returns, and claims not supported by evidence. Where abuse is identified, we may decline a return, withhold a refund, restrict return privileges, or suspend the account in accordance with our Terms of Service.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The policy in effect on the date your order was placed governs that order. Material changes will be notified in the app and reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
